# Code of Conduct

We want Sieve's community to be a place where people can do good technical work without being harassed or belittled. This is the Contributor Covenant 2.1 in short form.

## Our standard
Welcome: be kind, assume good faith, give and take feedback gracefully, focus on what is best for the project and its users, and credit others' work.

Not welcome: harassment, personal attacks, insults, sexualised language or attention, publishing others' private information, trolling, and other conduct a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening or harmful. This applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public.

Report problems privately to the maintainers through a GitHub security advisory marked as a conduct report, or by contacting the repository owner on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially and fairly.

Full text and the enforcement guidelines: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
